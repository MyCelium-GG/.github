# Code of conduct

MyCelium is a small hobby project. We want everyone who reports a bug, sends a
fix or asks a question to be treated well, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to modding or to GitHub.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what a mod does.
- Keep it on topic in issues and pull requests.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request after a clear no.

## Where this applies

All MyCelium-GG repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), the
MyCelium Discord server, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Email **mycelium.gg@gmail.com**, or message a moderator on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/8qgVgy526). Reports are read by the maintainers
and kept private.

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block people from the
project, temporarily or for good, depending on what happened. We will tell
the person why, unless that would put the reporter at risk.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
